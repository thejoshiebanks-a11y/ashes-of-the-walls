# Asset wishlist (what to send, in priority order)

Format: GLB (glTF 2.0) preferred, FBX accepted. Humanoids on a Mixamo-compatible skeleton so one animation set serves everyone.
Textures 1024 px or smaller. Send the source link and licence with every file (it goes in docs/CREDITS.md).
A credit satisfies CC-BY, but it does not license a model ripped from a game or anime. Keep the repo private until those are replaced.

## P0: needed for the Prologue (Shiganshina and the Breach)
- Child bodies (boy, girl) for the player, plus 2 adult bodies (a parent-figure Anchor).
- Canon cast, young: Eren, Mikasa, Armin. Adults: Hannes, Grisha Yeager, Carla Yeager.
- Civilians: 6 to 8 base bodies (men, women, elders, children) for crowds and fleeing refugees.
- Garrison soldier uniform and a few soldier bodies.
- Colossal Titan (very large, rigged, with the leg kick pose) and Armored Titan.
- Props: boats, wall rubble and debris pieces, crates and sacks (some already in the district).
- Animations: talking gestures, frantic run, stumble, crouch and hide, climb, push through a crowd, cry, sit.

## P1: Acts 1 and 2 (refugees, training)
Refugee camp kit (tents, carts, farm huts). Training grounds kit (barracks, mess hall, obstacle course, ODM tower, stables, horses).
Instructor Shadis. Cadets: Reiner, Bertholdt, Annie, Jean, Marco, Connie, Sasha, Krista, Ymir.

## P2: Acts 3 to 5 (Trost, expedition, Stohess)
Survey Corps, Garrison and Military Police uniforms with cloaks. Erwin, Hange, Mike, Petra, Oluo, Eld, Gunther.
Female Titan. 8 to 12 pure Titan variants. Forest of Giant Trees kit (huge trees). Stohess street kit. Horses.

## Audio (needs a licence note per file)
Music by mood: calm town, tension, battle, sorrow, triumph, plus a main-menu theme. SFX: ODM hook/reel/gas, blade hits, Titan footsteps and roars, wall impact and rubble, crowd panic, town ambience, UI sounds. Voice is optional later.

## Suggestions
Extra original civilians so the town feels lived in; a few original Garrison soldiers (cheap and avoids canon overload);
a CC0 music pack to prototype with before final tracks. Procedural effects (steam, dust, fire) are built in code and need no files.
