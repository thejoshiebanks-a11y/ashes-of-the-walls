    What came from your files

    Walls, pilasters and flared bases follow the proportions measured from your gate model. The gate is your model, placed unchanged. Houses are your medieval models (eight base types, varied by size, mirroring and tint). Eren’s home, the market stalls, cart, sacks, well, wall cannons and mossy boulders come from your second pack. The plaza houses, trees, barrels and cart come from your stage file.

    Built new

    Curved wall ring, deep stone canal with working barges and flowing water, outer canals, bridges, keep, bell towers, streets, an outer town, farmland and forests, and about 3,000 placed houses (nearby ones are full models, distant ones simplified). Hidden sides and the water gates are estimates, not measured.
